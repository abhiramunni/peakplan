About PeakPlan

Built by Abhiram Unni. PeakPlan matches task difficulty to your natural energy rhythm: demanding work lands in peak hours, light work fills the dips. One HTML file, no backend, no account, works offline.

How it works

1
Dump tasks into your inbox with a duration, priority and energy demand. "High energy" means deep-thinking work like writing or coding.
2
Tap Auto-Plan. PeakPlan slots demanding tasks into your peak hours, easy tasks into your dips, with 15-minute breathers between blocks.
3
Work the plan. Tap any block to start a distraction-free focus timer, mark it done, or move it. Your stats and streak update automatically.
